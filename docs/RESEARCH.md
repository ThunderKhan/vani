# Research Program

## Research thesis

Cross-layer coordination between speech uncertainty, critical-information safety, semantic message representation, and delay-tolerant routing can improve correct delivery of important multilingual instructions under constrained connectivity and device resources.

## Main research questions

1. Does critical-field-aware confirmation improve actionable meaning preservation?
2. How do ten-language model choices trade accuracy against latency, RAM, size, and energy?
3. Can uncertainty influence redundancy and acknowledgement policy usefully?
4. Can bounded priority-aware routing improve critical-message delivery per byte/energy?
5. When does semantic text outperform compressed audio under the same network?
6. Does endpointing dominate perceived latency on small devices?
7. Can one semantic bundle work across BLE, Wi-Fi, and a low-rate bridge without changing application semantics?

## Candidate contributions

- ten-language Android speech benchmark;
- critical-field/message-success metric;
- transport-independent bundle contract;
- uncertainty-aware confirmation;
- risk-aware bounded-copy routing;
- cross-layer evaluation on real devices.

## Scientific standards

- baselines;
- ablations;
- per-language breakdown;
- confidence intervals;
- device diversity;
- speaker-disjoint evaluation;
- reproducible configs;
- honest limitations;
- no copied paper numbers represented as project measurements.
