# Publication Plan

## Working title

**Critical-Information-Aware Semantic Speech Relaying over Disruption-Tolerant Android Meshes for Ten Indian Languages**

## Strong primary claim

Given uncertain on-device ASR and intermittent contact, allocating confirmation, redundancy, acknowledgement strength, copy budget, and receiver warnings according to semantic risk can improve correctly understood critical messages per byte or unit energy compared with transport-only policies.

## Minimum evidence

- two materially different Android device classes;
- all ten languages;
- speaker-disjoint evaluation;
- direct and multi-hop physical tests;
- human TTS intelligibility;
- compressed-audio baseline;
- routing baselines;
- ablations;
- licence audit;
- reproducible model conversion.

## Paper structure

1. Motivation
2. Related work
3. System model
4. Semantic bundle
5. Critical-field risk model
6. Routing policy
7. Android implementation
8. Experimental methodology
9. Results
10. Ablations
11. Limitations
12. Ethics/privacy
13. Reproducibility

## Claim discipline

Do not claim:
- guaranteed delivery;
- universal accuracy;
- production-grade cryptographic protocol without review;
- better-than-paper benchmarks without identical conditions;
- novel algorithm where only integration is new.
